Preventive Care & Daily Wellness Library

💧 Smart Hydration
Daily Habit

Drink 300–500 ml of water right after waking up to jumpstart metabolism. Spread intake evenly throughout the day rather than chugging late at night.

🧠 20-20-20 Eye Rule
Screen Health

Every 20 minutes of screen work, look at something 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds to prevent digital eye strain and tension headaches.

🛌 Circadian Rhythm
Sleep Hygiene

Keep your wake-up time consistent even on weekends, dim bright lights 60 minutes before bed, and aim for 7–9 hours of quality sleep.

🥗 Balanced Plate Method
Nutrition

Fill half your plate with colorful vegetables, one-quarter with lean protein, and one-quarter with whole grains or complex carbohydrates.

❤️ Heart Zone Movement
Cardio

Target at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week plus 2 days of strength training to support cardiovascular longevity.

🧘 Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)
Stress Relief

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale for 4 seconds, and hold for 4 seconds. Repeat 4 cycles to quickly calm your nervous system.
